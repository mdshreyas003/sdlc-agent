Your agent can read the code.
It can't read the backstory.

Every odd-looking line exists for a reason. whycode tells your agent that reason before it edits the line.

02  the idea

Store decisions, not documents.

A decision is one choice the team made: what, why, the exact sentence that says so, and when. Everything else in the graph exists to answer one question about it.

04  why trust it

Built to be checked, not believed.

Never quotes the dead

Overruled choices are labelled, so nobody restores what the team undid.

Says "I don't know"

No decision reaches the code? It refuses, and points to the nearest evidence and a person to ask.

Every claim is clickable

Each answer cites the PR, issue or doc it came from. Unchecked citations are dropped.

bottom line

Code tells you what it does. whycode tells your agent why, before it touches it.
